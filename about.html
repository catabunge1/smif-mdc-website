Our story

Years in the making.

In March 2022, Miami Dade College launched the Student Managed Investment Fund — a milestone brought to life through the founding support of TradeStation.

We equip students with hands-on experience in the global financial markets, with a focus on the stock exchange. Beyond investing, we build careers through internships, mentorship, and networking — helping students grow professionally and personally in the heart of Miami.

Our mission is to foster a collaborative environment that encourages innovation, resilience, and the pursuit of excellence in every aspect of finance. Whether you're crunching numbers, pitching investment ideas, or connecting with global professionals, SMIF is here to guide you every step of the way.

What guides us

Three guiding pillars.

01

Educating

Hands-on training in financial analysis, portfolio management, and team-based investing — turning theory into real decisions.

02

Growing

An immersive environment that builds the skills, confidence, and leadership our members need to thrive in the finance industry.

03

Giving back

Championing financial literacy, mentoring peers, and engaging in efforts that extend our impact beyond the classroom.
